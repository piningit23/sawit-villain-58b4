Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition
**The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue**.

`main.py` (Python 3.13 stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes one
100-word monologue to `/output/entry.txt`: a fantasy villain with a scheme
(the Ember Crown, the Ashen Gate) and three hundred years of history, whose
tiredness lives in the pauses, the lost thread and the kettle going on —
never in a plain statement that he is weary. It ends on the sting:
he keeps losing the thread just before he wins.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt (100 words, 521 characters)
```
